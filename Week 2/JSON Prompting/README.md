# Cultural Heritage & Festive AI Generation Project

This repository contains a curated collection of cinematic AI generation prompts, configuration parameters, and visual outputs highlighting traditional South Indian culture, art forms, and festive celebrations.

## Overview

The project explores high-fidelity text-to-image and text-to-video prompt engineering for capturing rich cultural details, traditional attire, architectural heritage, and warm devotional moods.

---

## 1. Classical Bharatanatyam Dancer (Image Generation)

### Prompt Configuration
```json
{
  "subject": "A classical Bharatanatyam dancer in an expressive Aramandi mid-pose, adorned in vibrant silk costume, temple jewellery, and intricate ghungroos",
  "setting": "An ancient stone-pillared mandapam of a South Indian temple with rich heritage carvings",
  "visual_style": "Cinematic portrait photography with sharp focus on the dancer's expressive mudras and costume details",
  "colour_palette": "Vibrant temple red, peacock green, rich gold zari, and sandstone brown",
  "lighting": "Dramatic warm golden hour sunlight streaming through the stone pillars, creating a halo effect",
  "mood": "Graceful, dynamic, devotional, and powerful",
  "composition": "Full-body wide shot, centered, emphasizing symmetry and sculptural posture",
  "aspect_ratio": "9:16"
}
```

### Visual Output Preview
- **Asset Name**: `IMAGE PROMPT 1 - Output.jpg`
- **Description**: A stunning vertical portrait showcasing the dancer in an authentic *Aramandi* posture inside a sunlit temple corridor.

## 2. Ganesh Chaturthi Family Prayer Ritual (Video Generation)

### Prompt Configuration
```json
{
  "subject": "The three children—an older girl in a deep purple embroidered dress, a toddler girl with a top-knot in a peach and purple pattu pavada, and a young boy in a cream shirt and veshti—joyfully participating in a Ganesh Chaturthi prayer ritual",
  "setting": "A traditional Tamil Nadu home living room decorated with a fresh mango leaf thoranam and glowing brass kuthuvilaku lamps",
  "visual_style": "Cinematic and heartwarming storytelling video with crisp detail",
  "colour_palette": "Festive marigold yellow, temple gold, deep purple, peach, and warm earthy tones",
  "lighting": "Warm, soft indoor glow from traditional oil lamps casting gentle highlights on the children",
  "mood": "Devotional, festive, joyous, and magical",
  "composition": "Medium full shot framing the three children together around the decorated clay Ganesha idol",
  "aspect_ratio": "16:9",
  "motion": "Continuous gentle camera pan moving from the glowing oil lamps to capture the smiling faces of the three children",
  "duration": "10 seconds"
}
```

### Visual Output Preview
- **Asset Name**: `VIDEO PROMPT` (10-second cinematic sequence)
- **Description**: A heartwarming frame capturing children celebrating Ganesh Chaturthi surrounded by traditional brass lamps and marigold garlands.
